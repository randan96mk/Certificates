## Exam Details

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Exam Code** | AD0-E907 |
| **Certification Level** | Expert |
| **Product** | Adobe Workfront |
| **Delivery** | Online proctored (Examity) or test center |
| **Language** | English |
| **Number of Questions** | 53 |
| **Question Format** | Multiple choice, multiple select, drag-and-drop, scenario-based |
| **Time Limit** | 106 minutes |
| **Passing Score** | 35/53 (~66%) |
| **Cost** | $225 USD ($150 USD in India) |
| **Validity** | 2 years (requires renewal) |
| **Prerequisite** | None officially required; Expert-level experience recommended |

## Who Should Take This Exam?

This certification is designed for practitioners with **deep hands-on experience** with Adobe Workfront, including:

- Workfront system administrators and developers
- Technical consultants implementing Workfront
- Solution architects designing Workfront workflows
- Integration developers (Fusion, API)
- Professionals who configure, customize, and extend Workfront

## Recommended Experience

- **2+ years** hands-on experience with Workfront administration and configuration
- Deep understanding of Workfront object hierarchy and relationships
- Experience with:
  - Custom forms, calculated fields, and display logic
  - Approval processes and milestone paths
  - Workfront Fusion scenario design
  - API and webhooks
  - Reporting and dashboards (text mode)
  - Resource management (Workload Balancer, Resource Planner)
  - Proofing and document management
  - Access levels, layout templates, and group administration

## Exam Domains at a Glance

| # | Domain | Weight | ~Questions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Core System Administration and Setup | 17% | ~9 |
| 2 | Intake, Custom Forms, and Project Initiation | 13% | ~7 |
| 3 | Strategic Functionality: Portfolio, Program & Resource Mgmt | 13% | ~7 |
| 4 | Document Management and Proofing | 13% | ~7 |
| 5 | Reporting | 11% | ~6 |
| 6 | Methodology / Best Practices / Use Cases | **22%** | ~12 |
| 7 | Business Consulting | 11% | ~6 |

> **Architect's Note:** Domain 6 (Methodology/Best Practices) is the heaviest at 22%. Combined with Business Consulting (11%), scenario-based thinking accounts for 33% of the exam. Focus on practical application, not just feature knowledge.

## Preparation Strategy

### Phase 1: Foundation (Week 1)
- Review all exam objectives and domain breakdown
- Self-assess current knowledge gaps
- Set up a Workfront sandbox for hands-on practice

### Phase 2: Deep Dive (Weeks 2-3)
- Study each domain systematically
- Complete hands-on exercises in sandbox
- Review official documentation for each topic
- Work through scenario-based questions

### Phase 3: Practice & Refine (Week 4)
- Take practice assessments
- Review weak areas
- Focus on Fusion and integration patterns
- Use revision checklists
