## Exam Details

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Exam Code** | AD0-E117 |
| **Certification Level** | Certified Master |
| **Product** | Adobe Experience Manager Sites |
| **Delivery** | Online proctored or test center |
| **Languages** | English, Japanese |
| **Number of Questions** | 50 |
| **Question Format** | Multiple choice & multiple select |
| **Time Limit** | 100 minutes |
| **Passing Score** | 29/50 (58%) |

| **Validity** | 2 years |
| **AEM Versions** | AEM 6.4, AEM 6.5, AEM as a Cloud Service |

## Who Should Take This Exam?

This is a **Master-level** certification designed for experienced architects:

- AEM Architects (3-5+ years of AEM experience)
- Lead Developers transitioning to architecture
- Technical Architects and Solution Architects
- Dev/Ops Lead Engineers

## Recommended Experience

- **3+ years** as an architect designing AEM Sites solutions
- Ability to design technical aspects of the AEM platform
- Experience with:
  - Designing software change processes (on-prem and cloud)
  - Integrating 3rd party and Adobe products with AEM
  - Defining migration and upgrade strategies
  - Understanding deployment methodologies (content and code)
  - Creating infrastructure/deployment diagrams
  - Determining information models for AEM applications
  - Defining authentication and authorization models
  - Determining persistence models (binary storage, deployment scenarios)

## Exam Domains at a Glance

| # | Domain | Weight | ~Questions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Discovery | 18% | ~9 |
| 2 | Solution Design | **44%** | ~22 |
| 3 | Implementation | 22% | ~11 |
| 4 | Maintenance | 16% | ~8 |

> **Architect's Note:** Domain 2 is nearly HALF the exam. Master solution design patterns, integration architecture, security, and migration strategies.

## Preparation Strategy

### Phase 1: Foundation (Week 1)
- Review exam blueprint and self-assess
- Identify gap areas in your AEM architecture knowledge
- Refresh on AEM as a Cloud Service differences

### Phase 2: Solution Design Deep Dive (Weeks 2-3)
- Focus on Domain 2 (44% of exam)
- Study: integration patterns, security models, persistence, caching
- Practice: architecture decision-making scenarios
- Review: AEM 6.5 vs AEMaaCS differences

### Phase 3: Practice & Refine (Week 4)
- Complete practice assessments
- Review community tips and common pitfalls
- Focus on scenario-based questions
- Use revision checklists
